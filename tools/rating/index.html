Rating web pages

About 5-7 minutes.

You will see 30 pictures of web pages, one at a time. For each one, imagine you had to use that page to get something done, and answer one question:

How overwhelming does this page feel?

Choose one of five answers, from "Not at all" to "Extremely", by pressing it on the screen or its number on the keyboard. Go with your first impression — there are no right answers. Some pages may come up more than once.
