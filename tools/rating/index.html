Rating web pages

You will see pictures of web pages, one at a time. For each one, imagine you had to use that page to get something done, and answer one question:

How overwhelming would this page be to use?

Answer from 1 (not at all overwhelming) to 7 (extremely overwhelming), by pressing a number on the screen or on the keyboard. Go with your first impression — there are no right answers. Some pages may come up more than once.
